STILL. · THE EDITORIAL COLOR STUDIO

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · Package: com.arhiem.still

1. 100% On-Device Processing

STILL. is an offline-first photo editing studio. All photographs you select, edit, crop, restore, or export are processed strictly on your device using local WebGL and Canvas graphics APIs. Your photos are never uploaded to external servers.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

STILL. does not require user accounts, does not collect personal information, does not access your contacts or location, and does not include third-party advertising SDKs or analytics trackers.

3. Local Storage & Preferences

Your bookmarked presets, custom color recipes, imported .CUBE LUT files, and editing preferences are stored locally on your device so they remain available offline. Clearing app data or uninstalling the application permanently removes these local records.

4. Photo Library Access

When you choose a photo to edit, STILL. uses your operating system's standard photo picker to read only the specific image(s) you explicitly select. Exported photos are saved only when you tap Export.

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries regarding STILL. (com.arhiem.still), please contact the developer via the Google Play Store listing support email.
